Recent activity

    Protected servers

    Server switches cover everyone; a Members rule overrides them for one person. Force keeps a member server-muted/deafened and re-applies it if it is removed. Aegis needs Mute Members, Deafen Members and (for role commands) Manage Roles, with its role above the members and roles it manages.

    Roles

    Every role in your server, highest first. Move roles up and down, rename them, change their colour, or create and delete them without opening Discord. Aegis needs Manage Roles, and its own role above the roles it changes.
